Pixora Studio

Professional Photo Editing. Free for Everyone.

Layers, masks, adjustment layers, layer styles, typography, vectors, brushes, and true PSD support — in a native desktop application written entirely in Rust. Free, open source, offline, and yours.

Download for Windows View source on GitHub
100% Rust GPU-accelerated (wgpu) True PSD fidelity No telemetry · offline

Built for professionals

Menus, shortcuts, and panels are right where professional editors expect them — without subscriptions, accounts, or the cloud.

🎛️

Familiar by Design

From Ctrl+J to Ctrl+T, your Photoshop muscle memory works out of the box: same menus, tools, and dockable panels.

⚡

Native & Lightning Fast

Pure Rust with a GPU compositor on wgpu (DirectX 12 and Vulkan), tile-based multithreading, and copy-on-write memory. Zero Electron, zero webviews.

🗂️

True PSD Fidelity

Open, edit, and save layered Photoshop documents (PSD/PSB) with high-fidelity preservation of layers, blend modes, masks, and smart objects.

🔒

100% Offline & Free

No subscriptions, no accounts, no cloud dependencies, zero telemetry. Pixora Studio runs completely on your machine and belongs to you.

🖼️

Color-managed precision

Full ICC workflows on 8-bit, 16-bit integer and 32-bit float pipelines — RGB, Grayscale, CMYK and Lab.

🤖

Automation-first

Headless CLI, Photoshop-compatible Actions, and every feature exposed as an engine command — scriptable and agent-drivable.
